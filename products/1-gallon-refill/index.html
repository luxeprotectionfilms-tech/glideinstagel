How to use

  1. 1

    Apply

    On a clean, cool surface out of direct sun, apply a light, even amount where controlled positioning is needed.

  2. 2

    Position

    Place and adjust the film with controlled movements. Avoid forcing dry tack too early.

  3. 3

    Squeegee

    Center-out, consistent pressure, overlapping strokes; move excess toward the edges.

  4. 4

    Finish

    Remove excess, keep edges clean and dry, then complete edge and tack work per your film’s process.

Application amount can affect working feel and positioning control. Test and adjust for your film, panel and process. GLIDE is your slip, not your tack.
