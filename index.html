How it works

Apply. Position. Squeegee. Finish.

  1. 1

    Apply

    On a clean, cool surface out of direct sun, apply a light, even amount where controlled positioning is needed.

  2. 2

    Position

    Place and adjust the film with controlled movements. Avoid forcing dry tack too early.

  3. 3

    Squeegee

    Work center-out with consistent pressure and overlapping strokes, moving excess toward the edges.

  4. 4

    Finish

    Remove excess, keep edges clean and dry, then complete edge and tack work per your film’s process.

Application amount can affect working feel and positioning control. Test and adjust for your film, panel and process.

GLIDE is your slip, not your tack. Use your preferred tack solution for edges, returns and high-tension areas.

FAQ

Is GLIDE a slip solution?

Yes. GLIDE is a premixed professional PPF installation gel used at the slip and positioning stage of a wet install.

Does GLIDE replace tack solution?

No. Tack requirements depend on the film, the panel and your workflow. Keep using your preferred tack solution where you need it.

How many 32 oz bottles does a gallon refill?

Can I use GLIDE with other PPF brands?

GLIDE is made for professional PPF installation. Test it on your film and follow the film manufacturer’s installation requirements.

Is GLIDE hazardous?
